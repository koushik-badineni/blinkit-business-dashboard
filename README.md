# 🛒 Blinkit Business Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI business dashboard designed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, product performance, outlet operations, sales trends, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project simulates a real-world Data Analyst scenario at **Blinkit**, one of India's leading quick-commerce companies.

The objective is to transform the Blinkit Grocery dataset into an interactive and insight-driven Power BI dashboard that helps management understand:

- Product performance
- Outlet performance
- Sales trends
- Customer satisfaction
- Item visibility
- Fat content impact
- Tier-wise outlet performance
- Top-performing products

The dashboard is designed to support business decision-making through KPIs, interactive visualizations, slicers, drill-through navigation, and DAX measures.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Blinkit's management needs to understand several business challenges:

- Certain product categories may be underperforming despite high demand.
- Outlet performance varies across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities.
- Sales may be affected by item visibility and fat content.
- Customer satisfaction and repeat purchases require monitoring.

The dashboard addresses these business requirements through interactive analysis and visualization. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze overall sales performance.
2. Identify top-performing product categories and items.
3. Compare outlet performance across different tiers.
4. Analyze sales by outlet type.
5. Analyze the impact of fat content on sales.
6. Study sales trends over time.
7. Analyze the relationship between item visibility and sales.
8. Monitor customer satisfaction using ratings.
9. Identify high-visibility but low-sales products.
10. Provide management with actionable business insights.
